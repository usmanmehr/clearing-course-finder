Frequently asked questions

Plain English, no jargon. If something here does not make sense, that is our fault, not yours - let us know.

How your UCAS points are worked out

When you enter your grades, we convert them into UCAS Tariff points using the official UCAS Tariff tables - the same tables universities themselves use. For A-levels each grade is worth a fixed number of points:

  • A* = 56 points
  • A = 48 points
  • B = 40 points
  • C = 32 points
  • D = 24 points
  • E = 16 points

We add up your best three to give your total. For example: Economics A (48) + History A (48) + Maths B (40) = 136 points, an AAB profile. BTECs convert on the same official tables, so a BTEC or a mix of BTECs and A-levels is counted on exactly the same points scale. This points figure is exact - it is proper maths, not an estimate.

Why we don't show a course's required grades

We do not hold live, course-level entry requirements. What a university asks for varies from course to course, and it can change during Clearing itself as places fill up. Because of that, we do not display a per-course grade requirement - showing one would risk being wrong, and the last thing you want on Results Day is a number that sends you in the wrong direction.

Use our ranking to build a shortlist of universities worth contacting, then always confirm the real requirement on UCAS or by calling the university's clearing hotline on Results Day. A person on the phone can tell you exactly what is available and what grades they will accept, right now, today.

Why does the "Clearing page" link not work sometimes?

Universities move and rename their clearing pages fairly often - especially right before and during Clearing, when they are updating things constantly. We check every university's clearing page automatically once a day (more often on Results Day itself), so if a link has stopped working, we should catch it and warn you rather than just sending you to a dead page.

If you see a warning that a link "could not be loaded" - trust it, and use the phone number instead. If a link looks like it is there but has a note saying our check "could not confirm" it is working, that usually just means the university's website is blocking automated visits (quite a few do this on purpose to stop bots) - it will very likely work fine when you click it yourself in a normal browser.

Either way, the phone number is always your most reliable option on Results Day. Websites can be slow, wrong, or overloaded with traffic - a real person on the clearing hotline can tell you exactly what is available right now.

Is any of this guaranteed?

No, and we do not want to pretend otherwise. This is a free, independent tool built to help you find a good shortlist of universities and courses quickly - not an official UCAS service, and not connected to UCAS's live data. Course availability, phone lines, and offers can all change without us knowing. Always double-check directly with the university before making any decisions.

What information do you keep about me?

Only what is needed to show you results and let you export or share them - your search (grades, subject, filters) is stored for 30 minutes so the "export" and "share this search" links work, then it is deleted automatically. We do not ask for your name, email, or any account details to use the search tool itself. We do log some technical details (like a rough approximate area from your IP address, and which browser or device type you are using) to understand how the site is used and to keep it running smoothly - not to identify you personally.

Something is wrong or confusing - who do I tell?

Please do get in touch - feedback@mehrs.net. This is built and run by one person, not a big team, so real feedback from students genuinely helps make it better for the next person.
