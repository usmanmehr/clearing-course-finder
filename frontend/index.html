What's exact vs estimated

Exact: your UCAS Tariff points. We convert your A-level and BTEC grades using the official published UCAS Tariff tables - the same points a university admissions system uses.

Estimated: everything about course availability. "Typical offer" bands, and whether a course is open, are based on each university's overall tier and Clearing status, not this year's live vacancy data for the specific course - a real UCAS Clearing feed for that doesn't exist here yet. Always confirm directly with the university before relying on it.

Salary is the national median for the subject (HESA Graduate Outcomes 2022/23), not a university-specific figure. Graduate prospects, where shown, are from the Complete University Guide 2027. Live vacancies are confirmed by phone on Results Day in August 2026.
